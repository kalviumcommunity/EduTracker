# Analysis Visualizations

## Chart 1: Revenue by Product Line
- **Type:** Horizontal bar chart
- **Question:** Which product line generates the most revenue last quarter?
- **Key Insight:** Product A is the top performer with the highest revenue share.
- **Annotation:** Highlights the top-performing product line.

## Chart 2: Revenue Trend by Top Products
- **Type:** Line chart with multiple series
- **Question:** How has revenue evolved for the top 3 products over the last 12 months?
- **Key Insight:** Product A shows consistent upward momentum; Product B is growing steadily; Product C is stable.
- **Annotation:** Marks the peak month for Product A.

## Chart 3: Order Value Distribution
- **Type:** Histogram
- **Question:** What is the distribution of order values?
- **Key Insight:** Most orders fall below $100, with secondary clusters in the $200-$300 and $500-$600 ranges.
- **Annotation:** Identifies where the largest concentration of orders occurs.

## Chart 4: Quarterly Revenue Composition
- **Type:** Stacked bar chart
- **Question:** How does product line composition contribute to quarterly revenue?
- **Key Insight:** Product A is increasingly dominant, while Product C remains a smaller share.
- **Annotation:** Highlights Product A's growing share of revenue.

## Chart 5: Marketing Spend vs Revenue
- **Type:** Scatter plot with trend line
- **Question:** Does marketing spend correlate with revenue generation?
- **Key Insight:** There is a positive relationship, though some months underperform relative to spend.
- **Annotation:** Marks the month with high spend but lower-than-expected revenue.

## Colour Palette
- Primary: `#1f77b4` (blue) - main data series
- Secondary: `#ff7f0e` (orange) - comparative highlights
- Success: `#2ca02c` (green) - positive or growth signals
- Warning: `#d62728` (red) - outliers, anomalies, risk areas
- Neutral: `#7f7f7f` (gray) - base chart elements

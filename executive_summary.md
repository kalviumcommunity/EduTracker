# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE
## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is our leading revenue loss driver, costing approximately $2M annually. Our current churn rate is 7%, which is well above the industry average of 4%. This analysis identifies a clear operational lever that can reduce churn quickly and protect our most valuable customers.

### Key Findings
- **Support speed directly impacts retention.** Customers who receive support within 2 hours churn at 3%, while customers waiting more than 24 hours churn at 12%.
- **Current support response averages 6 hours.** That gap is the primary retention bottleneck and is directly tied to lost customers.
- **High-value customers are most exposed.** Customers spending more than $10K per year churn at 15% when support is slow, making this group especially urgent.

### Business Risks
- **Revenue at risk:** At the current churn rate of 7%, our business is losing $2M in annual recurring revenue.
- **High-value customer risk:** The top-spending customer segment is churning at 15%, meaning one lost large account could erase the benefit of an entire year of support hiring.
- **Competitive vulnerability:** Competitors with faster support are recruiting our customers, making churn not just a retention issue but a strategic risk.
- **Operational burnout:** Support response times are worsening even as volume rises, suggesting the team is overloaded and quality may continue to decline.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (Cost: $200K/year)
   - Reduces average response time from 6 hours to under 2 hours.
   - Expected impact: recover approximately $400K in churn reduction.
   - ROI: roughly 2x within the first year.
2. **Implement a <2 hour response SLA** (Cost: $0)
   - Document the target and track it daily.
   - Creates accountability and ensures support prioritizes the right requests.
   - Implementation date: January 1.
3. **Prioritize High-Value Customers** (Cost: $50K engineering)
   - Route customers spending more than $10K/year to a dedicated support lane.
   - Expected impact: reduce high-value customer churn by 50%.
   - Timeline: February 1.

### Decision Needed
Approve the hiring budget of $200K and the engineering investment of $50K by December 15. This will enable the changes needed to capture an estimated $400K in churn recovery in year one.
